SafeRoute VR — Fire Emergency & Evacuation Training Simulator

SafeRoute VR is a virtual reality training simulator developed in Unity to help users understand and practice fire emergency response and building evacuation procedures in a simulated indoor environment.

The project recreates a building emergency scenario in which the primary exit is blocked by fire and the elevator is unavailable. Trainees must recognize hazards, follow evacuation guidance toward an alternative exit, and reach the designated assembly point.

Key Features

- Dynamic Fire Hazards:Multiple fire and smoke locations simulate an evolving emergency scenario.
- Evacuation Guidance:Guides trainees away from blocked exits and fire danger zones toward a safer evacuation route.
- VR Interaction:Built with Unity and XR Interaction Toolkit, with XR Device Simulator support for development and testing without a physical headset.
- Emergency Environment:Includes fire safety equipment, emergency signage, environmental materials, and simulated building interiors.
- Failure and Restart System:Demonstrates the consequences of approaching dangerous fire zones and supports scenario replay.
- Training-Oriented Design:Focuses on hazard awareness, emergency decision-making, and evacuation procedures.

Technology Stack

- Unity 2022.3 LTS
- C#
- Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
- XR Interaction Toolkit
- XR Device Simulator

Project Objective

The goal of SafeRoute VR is to provide an immersive and repeatable environment for practicing emergency response decisions without exposing trainees to real-world fire hazards.

Note:SafeRoute VR is an educational simulation and is not a substitute for certified fire safety training or official emergency evacuation procedures.
